# Ohm's Law – Virtual AB Experiment

## About the Project
This project is a Unity-based virtual laboratory experiment designed to demonstrate **Ohm's Law** in an interactive 3D environment. It helps students understand the relationship between voltage, current, and resistance through virtual electrical circuit experiments.

## Objectives
- Understand the principle of Ohm's Law.
- Explore the relationship between voltage, current, and resistance.
- Perform a virtual electrical circuit experiment.
- Observe voltage and current variations.
- Improve practical learning through simulation.

## Ohm's Law
Ohm's Law states that the current flowing through a conductor is directly proportional to the voltage applied across it, provided the temperature and other physical conditions remain constant.

**Formula:**

`V = I × R`

Where:
- **V** – Voltage (Volts)
- **I** – Current (Amperes)
- **R** – Resistance (Ohms)

## Experiment Procedure
1. Open the Unity project.
2. Launch the virtual Ohm's Law experiment scene.
3. Set the required voltage and resistance values.
4. Observe the corresponding current value.
5. Change the voltage or resistance and observe the changes.
6. Record the readings and verify Ohm's Law.
